So, you've decided to implement NNUE for your engine. It's important to start with a simple network at first and then increase its complexity while testing every change. The usual progression is:
1. [Basic NNUE](<Basic articles/Basic NNUE.md>) (debugging [data](<Debugging data/Basic NNUE debugging data.md>))
2. [Output buckets](<Basic articles/Output buckets.md>)
3. [Mirroring](<Basic articles/Mirroring.md>)
4. [Input buckets](<Basic articles/Input buckets.md>)
5. [Multilayer](<Basic articles/Multilayer.md>) (debugging [data](<Debugging data/Multilayer debugging data.md>))
You also can do different [optimizations](<Optimizations and tricks/Optimizations.md>) and [tricks](<Optimizations and tricks/NNUE tricks.md>).
