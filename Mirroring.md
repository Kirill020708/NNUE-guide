Chess is a symmetrical game, so we can divide the board into two halves. The first half is the first 4 columns (a-d) and the second half is columns (e-h). If the king is on the second half, we can mirror the position across `d/e` boundary. The only problem is when the king goes from one half to another, we need to recalculate the whole net from scratch because all of the pieces change their location. But this happens rarely enough to not cause much slowdown. This technique gives our network a sense of symmetry, thus improving its quality.
